# Meniga

> Meniga is a global leader in digital banking and personal finance management (PFM) solutions, serving over 100 million banking customers across 30 countries. Meniga provides RESTful APIs and mobile SDKs on top of its software products so banks can enrich transaction data and deliver personalized finance-management, cashflow-forecasting, smart-savings, sustainability, and agentic-banking experiences inside their own digital channels. The API is white-label and tenant-hosted (each bank operates its own Meniga API URL) and secured with OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect via Meniga's IdentityServer stack.

This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from Meniga's public developer surface (2026-07-20). Meniga does not publish an llms.txt of its own; its developer portal is access-gated.

## Products
- [Transaction Enrichment](https://www.meniga.com/products/enrichment/): categorize and clean raw bank transaction data
- [Finance Management (PFM)](https://www.meniga.com/products/finance-management/): personalized money management
- [Cashflow Forecasting](https://www.meniga.com/products/cashflow-forecasting/): predict future balances and cashflow
- [Insights](https://www.meniga.com/products/insights/): personalized banking insights and nudges
- [Open Banking](https://www.meniga.com/products/open-banking/): PSD2 account-information aggregation
- [Smart Savings](https://www.meniga.com/products/smart-savings/): automated goal-based savings
- [Sustainability](https://www.meniga.com/products/sustainability/): carbon-footprint insights on spending

## SDKs
- [iOS SDK — MenigaSDK](https://cocoapods.org/pods/MenigaSDK): `pod 'MenigaSDK'` — docs at https://meniga.github.io/mobile-sdk-ios/
- [Android SDK — com.meniga.sdk:sdk-android](https://mvnrepository.com/artifact/com.meniga.sdk/sdk-android): Maven Central — docs at https://meniga.github.io/mobile-sdk-android/
- [Meniga.IdentityModel](https://github.com/meniga/Meniga.IdentityModel): .NET Standard OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect helper

## Authentication
- OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect via Meniga IdentityServer; authorization/token endpoints are tenant-specific per financial institution.
